Motion · five rules
  1. One thing moves at a time.
  2. Steps last about 3 seconds, with Pause.
  3. Things arrive as they reach the middle of the screen.
  4. Scrolling moves you through the day: night → morning → day.
  5. Reduced motion shows the finished picture, still.

Kept from what you approved: the headline, "Connect Gmail, start free" and "See how it works", the four promises, the wash, white app screens, the orange dot. Never: a dashboard as the first picture, a person photo, handwriting, a black reply card.
