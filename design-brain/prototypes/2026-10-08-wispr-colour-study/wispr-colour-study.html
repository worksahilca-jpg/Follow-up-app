How Wispr Flow uses colour

Few colours. Each one has a single job.

Wispr looks professional because it uses only a handful of colours and never mixes their jobs. Warm paper for reading, one deep colour for weight, one soft colour that only ever means “press me”.

Cream paper #FFFFEB

The page itself: warm, like paper, never pure white.

Near-black #1A1A1A

Words. Big serif headlines, plain sans text.

Deep teal-green #034F46

The heavy blocks: dark sections and panels that give the cream contrast.

Lavender #F0D7FF

Only one job: “press me”. Every main button, nothing else.

Warm orange #FFA946

Tiny warm highlights, used rarely.

Wine #7F1C34

A rare deep accent in a few cards.

  • Paper, not whiteA warm cream page feels like a journal, not a dashboard. It calms the page down.
  • One heavy colour, used in blocksWhole sections turn deep green, with very round corners, and slide over each other as you scroll. That is where the depth comes from.
  • The button colour is never used for anything elseLavender only appears on things you press, so your eye learns it in one second.
  • Warm on purposeThey chose warm colours to stand apart from the cold blue-and-purple look of most AI tools.
Why they did it, in their own words

It worked in the app, but nobody remembered it.

“The palette worked well in product, but fell short in the wild. It wasn’t recognizable in a feed, and it wasn’t memorable in a moment.”Wispr, “Rebranding Flow”
“Soft neutrals and thoughtful contrast lean away from the clinical palettes common in AI startups.”Wispr, “Rebranding Flow”
“Every visual, every blur, every curve or cue echoes the same idea: voice, in motion.”Wispr, “Rebranding Flow”
They looked at icy blues and purples with gradient overlays (“precision, technology, the future”) and went warm instead. The whole rebrand took six weeks, done by about two and a half people in-house.Same post
Gradients

Wispr is mostly flat. The softness comes from blur, not gradients.

Their sections are solid blocks of one colour. What feels soft is blurred photography behind the product cards. The gradient-heavy site is Superhuman: every product has its own gradient mood. So gradients can work for us, if we keep them to one colour family and use them like light.

Light falling on a blockOne colour, lighter where the light hits. Adds depth, stays calm.
One-colour fadeMint to sage. Soft, fresh, still one colour.
Many colours at oncePeach, blue and pink together: our current theme, the one you didn’t like.
Neon / rainbowThe cheap AI-startup look Wispr ran away from.
