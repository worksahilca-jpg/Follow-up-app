Weak spots, honestly

What I'd still fix.

  • On the desk, "Alerts" shortens the business name in the sidebar ("Maple Re…"). We could keep the word on the phone only.
  • The local account has only "Needs you" customers, so Checking in, Qualified and Booked show here in the words table, not on a screen.
  • Settings isn't redrawn. It's already one list (A-069); it only needs the same headline and words.
  • The display face is a new font in the app. It is already on the homepage, so visitors have usually loaded it once.
