Is this lead ready? The qualification card

One card on each customer's page. It shows what FollowUp knows about them, the customer's own words behind every answer, what it will ask next, and when it hands them to you. Realtor version, built on the six things agents already ask: location, price, why they're moving, agent, mortgage and a viewing.

1 · Still learning

On the customer's page, beside the conversation

The card sits at the top of the right-hand column, above "Came from". Every tick has her words under it, highlighted in the thread too. Nothing is guessed: if she hasn't said it, it says "Not yet". If she said something unclear, it says "Not sure" and shows why.

2 · Ready

Hot: booked and handed to you, with why

When every must-have is known, the card turns green, the viewing is booked, and you get one alert: "Nadia is ready". The "Why" line is in her own words, so you can check it in three seconds. One tap tells FollowUp whether it was right. That tap is how it gets more accurate.

3 · Your rules

What makes a lead hot: you decide, once

In Settings. FollowUp starts from what agents already ask, and you switch things on or off. "Ask about it" means FollowUp may ask, gently, one question at a time, always after answering theirs. "Must-have" means it won't call a lead hot without it.

What makes a lead hot

For your business. Change it any time; it applies to new messages.
