# Apps the founder loves: Macro, Granola, Wispr Flow, Follow Up Boss, Attio, monday.com, Superhuman (2026-10-05)

**Why:** the founder asked for lessons from same-industry CRMs, good-UX CRMs, productivity tools he likes ("specially
macro dot com, I love it"), Wispr Flow's colours and Granola. **Evidence, honestly:** grade B. Mobbin needs a paid plan,
and macro.com and wisprflow.ai are blocked from this environment, so nothing here comes from clicking through the apps:
it comes from their own design write-ups, docs and reviews (sources below). Principles only; no screen is reproduced
(S-16).

| App | What it does well | Principle for FollowUp | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|---|
| Macro | One inbox for email, chat, mentions and tasks; fast | One list, every channel | A-087 (Today as one list) |
| Granola | The user's notes are black and the AI's are grey; the AI stays quiet in the meeting | Always show who wrote what; the AI helps, the owner steers | Check-up #18 (not built) |
| Wispr Flow | Rebrand from "competent but cold" to "grounded and human": cream, black, one soft action colour | Warmth makes an AI tool feel human; one colour means "press me" | Check-up #20, founder's call (a `[TO DECIDE]` token change) |
| Follow Up Boss | Smart lists that refill themselves ("no contact in 30 days") become the agent's day | The list decides who to call | Check-up #17 (round 2) |
| Attio | Power shows only when reached for | Simple on top, depth one click away | Already A-080 (Details) |
| monday.com | Status colours with one meaning (stuck, working, done) | Colour only for status, same meaning everywhere | Check-up #19 |
| Superhuman | Built for the empty inbox; short replies | The goal of Today is "you're done" | Already A-046 |

**Not borrowed:** keyboard-first speed (Macro, Superhuman). R-002 closes it: the owner is on a phone between jobs.
