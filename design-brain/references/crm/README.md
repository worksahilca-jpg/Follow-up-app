# References — CRM

Lead and contact lists, pipelines, record detail pages, tables, filtering, bulk actions.

## What to look for here

- **Row anatomy.** What earns a place in a list row, and what's deferred to the detail
  view or revealed on hover.
- **How status is communicated** — color, label, position, or a combination. Whether it
  survives being colorblind.
- **Sorting and filtering that a non-technical user can actually operate.** Most CRM
  filtering is built for power users and abandoned by everyone else.
- **The list → detail transition.** Does context survive? Can you move to the next record
  without going back?
- **Bulk actions** — how they're made powerful without being dangerous.
- **Scale behavior.** What 10 rows looks like vs. 10,000.

## The FollowUp-specific question

Our lead list must make *"which of these is about to go cold"* visible without the user
sorting, filtering, or thinking. Most CRMs make the user do that work. Look for references
that do the thinking for the user — and note how they avoid feeling presumptuous.

## Anti-patterns to notice and name

Twenty columns; every row with six action buttons; filter builders with AND/OR logic
trees; "Add lead" as the most prominent action on a screen full of neglected leads;
pipelines that require dragging on a phone.
