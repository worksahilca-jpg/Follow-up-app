# Aer "Work Anywhere" hero concept — founder-named reference, 2026-09-18

**Source.** A single-screen concept for the bag brand Aer (a dribbble-style shot the founder
pasted: "let's use this"). Not a live site; the pasted image is the whole reference.

**What he wanted from it, in the end: the colour. Nothing else.** His words, after a first
cut that took the layout too: "I just wanted to use the colour, that's it" and "no person,
I want same as previous." So the page stays exactly as approved (A-011, the flat
black-and-white page with the lead-flow hero) and only the dark-mode ground changes from
pure black to the shot's charcoal.

**The colour, measured from the shot:** outer page ground about `#1b1b1d`, the hero panel
about `#28282a`, a slightly lighter strip about `#2c2c2e`. Applied as `--bg: #1e1e20`,
`--card: #27272a`, `--card-2: #2e2e31` in the ink token set; every other token unchanged.
Light mode is untouched (white).

**For the record, what is on the screen** (so no future session rebuilds it by mistake): a
framed charcoal panel with a narrow left rail (hamburger, vertical social links), a small
top row with an underlined active link, a hooded figure from behind carrying the screen, an
index block "/ 01" on its left, a two-line headline on its right, and a three-cell strip
(events, a small photo, news) along the bottom. A full cut of that layout with a generated
photo of a person was built and rejected within minutes (R-011: "doesn't make sense with
FollowUp"), then the layout itself was dropped at the founder's clarification above. Do not
propose the layout or a photographed person again.
