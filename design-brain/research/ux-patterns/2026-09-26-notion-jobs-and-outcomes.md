# Notion: use-case storytelling, and jobs and outcomes over capabilities

**Asked by:** the founder, 2026-09-26: *"Notion ... use case storytelling ... explain jobs and outcome instead of
technical capabilities ... see where we can implement these things for our product."*
**Method:** WebSearch summaries; notion.com can't be fetched from this session.
**Grades:** B = search summary of Notion's own pages or a named teardown; C = third-party write-up.

## What Notion does

| Principle | How Notion does it | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| **The headline is the outcome** | "The AI workspace that works for you." "Meet the night shift. Notion agents keep work moving 24/7." It sells work getting done, not blocks and databases. | B / C (2026 homepage write-ups) |
| **Use cases by job and role** | Use-case pages and a template gallery organised by what you're trying to do: onboarding, planning, a team wiki. | B (notion.com use-case pages) |
| **Personal first run** | Sign-up asks what you'll use it for and whether it's for you or a team, then offers about 5 templates that fit. | B (Candu, GoodUX / Appcues) |
| **Customer stories as before → after** | Challenge, then what changed, then a number: tools replaced, hours saved, fewer queries. | B (notion.com/customers) |
| **Learn by doing** | Templates are finished examples of an outcome. You start from the result and edit it, not from a blank page and a manual. | B (GoodUX / Appcues) |

## Where FollowUp stands

- **Already outcome-led:** the hero ("Never lose a lead because you forgot to follow up."), "Which customer am I about to lose?",
  and the weekly email's win ("Tom Reid came back and booked").
- **Still capability-led:**
  - The "Three steps" section: "It reads every conversation", "It writes the reply".
  - The "Works with" list.
  - Parts of the app's wording: "Automation off", "Workflows", "Sources".

## What to build from it

1. **Three short stories by trade, clearly labelled as examples.** One scene each:
   - a plumber under a sink at 7 pm;
   - a realtor at a showing;
   - a salon owner mid-cut.

   Each story tells the moment a customer writes, what FollowUp did, and how it ended ("booked Thursday"). They're
   examples, not testimonials, until real testers give us words (never fake a testimonial).
2. **Rewrite "Three steps" as jobs, not machinery:**
   - "Every customer gets an answer";
   - "You see who's slipping away";
   - "You only step in when it matters".
3. **One first-run question: "What kind of business?"** The answer tailors the examples, the plan's wording and the
   proof screen. This is Notion's personal first run, kept to one question (the Stripe minimum).
4. **App wording from jobs:**
   - "Automation off" → "FollowUp won't follow up with this customer";
   - "Workflows" → "Follow-up plans";
   - "Sources" → "Where customers write".

   The phone designs already do this; the live app doesn't yet.
5. **Real stories later, in Notion's shape:** before, what changed, a real number, only from testers who agree.

## Not taken

- Notion's "AI workspace" and "agents" language. For FollowUp, AI is an invisible capability, never a personality
  (CLAUDE.md).
- Template galleries. An owner doesn't want to choose a template; they want the replies written.
