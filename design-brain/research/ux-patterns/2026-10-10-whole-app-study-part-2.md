**Evidence grade:** A for the counts, which come from our own records. The populations are small (12 accounts,
including the founder's own test accounts), so read them as signs. Page analytics are off, so it isn't known which
screens are used or on what device.

## The biggest fact

- In 30 days, 306 replies were held for an OK.
- Owners sent 13: 11 as written and 2 edited. 8 more went out on their own (thank-yous and check-ins).
- 46 replies are waiting right now, in 5 accounts.
- 7 app opens were recorded since counting began (early October), across 4 accounts.

So **the reply is usually right; the step that fails is the owner reaching it.** The app is built as a place to visit,
and owners aren't visiting.

## Who uses it

- 11 accounts are set up: 7 real estate, 2 Other, 1 marketing agency, 1 legal.
- 9 are one person or didn't say. 2 said they have a team.
- All 11 are on Assisted; none chose Automatic.
- 49 customers in all, 42 of them in 2 accounts.
- Messages in, last 30 days: email 72, Instagram 11, website 6, Messenger 3, WhatsApp 2.
- **Instagram:** 13 connects and 14 disconnects, in 2 accounts.
- **Gmail:** 5 accounts ever connected; 2 work now and 2 need reconnecting.
- **Feedback box:** used once.

## The jobs, by how often

**Several times a day, on the phone:**
1. **(core)** Know if anyone needs me, without opening the app to check.
2. **(core)** Answer the one who needs me.
3. Not now, already handled, or don't send.

**A few times a week:**

4. Find one customer.
5. Add someone who called or walked in.
6. Take over a hot lead and close.

**About weekly:**

7. See that it's working.
8. Trust what it did while I was away.

**Rarely or once:**

9. Get started.
10. Teach a fact.
11. Change how it works, or stop it.
12. Bring in the team.
13. Account things.

**When something goes wrong:**

14. Fix a broken connection.
15. Tell someone.

**Never the owner's job** (FollowUp does these): catch, tell customers from noise, reply in their words and language,
check in, qualify, book, the 30-minute "we got you", learn facts.
