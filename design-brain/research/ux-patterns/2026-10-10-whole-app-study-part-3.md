## Why written replies wait (live, last 30 days)

| Step | Count |
|---|---|
| Replies held for an OK | 306 |
| Bell items created | 304 (66 read) |
| Alert emails | 20 |
| Phone alerts | 8, to the one phone that ever turned them on |
| App opens (person-days, since early October) | 7 |
| Owner sends | 13 (11 unchanged) |

**What fails:** the owner is never really told. The bell only works inside the app, and phone alerts reach one phone.

**Fix options, which combine** (B and C change behaviour, so they are the founder's call):
- **A.** Bring the decision to the owner: a phone alert or a text that opens that one reply. Never a Send inside an
  email.
- **B.** Ask less: only price, date or tense moments wait.
- **C.** Recommend Automatic, with the 11-of-13 proof beside it.

## The five problems

1. The app waits for the owner, and the owner doesn't come.
2. One customer looks different in every place.
3. One decision is split across several screens.
4. Settings is most of the app, and almost nobody uses it.
5. Good things are hidden.

Part 4 studies how familiar apps handle each of these.
