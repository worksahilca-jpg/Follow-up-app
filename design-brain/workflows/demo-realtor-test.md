# Test FollowUp ourselves: the demo realtor account (2026-10-21)

**Why:** the founder messaged 5 realtors; all said no, too busy. Until one says yes, we play both sides: a demo
realtor account that receives real emails and Instagram messages, and us writing to it as customers. It finds what
breaks before a realtor does. It does not tell us whether realtors want it; only a real one can.

## Setup (the founder does these; Claude can't create accounts)

1. **A new Google account just for the demo realtor** (a made-up name, e.g. "Maya Demo Realty").
2. **Sign up to FollowUp with it.** Pick Real estate. Connect its Gmail.
3. **A test Instagram professional account**, connected to a Facebook Page. While Meta's review is pending, it must
   be added as a tester in the Meta app before it can connect. Then connect it in Settings.
4. **Customers:** each fake customer needs their own email address or Instagram account. Use your own, and ask 2–3
   friends or family to send one message each from the list below. Made-up names only.

Leave the demo account on the default (every reply waits for your tap) unless a test says otherwise.

## The tests

Send each message, wait a few minutes, then look at Today. Mark ✓ or ✗ and write one line on anything odd.

### Email

| # | The customer writes | What should happen | ✓ / ✗ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Hi, I saw your listing at 24 Maple Crescent. Is it still available? We'd like to see it this weekend." | Shows on Today with a reply that answers and asks at most two things (area, when). Nothing goes out until you tap Send. | |
| 2 | "What's the asking price, and are they open to offers?" | The reply never makes up a price. It waits for you (a short "let me check" may go out if you leave it 30 minutes). | |
| 3 | "Thinking of selling my townhouse in Brampton next spring. What's it worth?" | Treated as a seller: offers a visit or a call, no number. | |
| 4 | "Just browsing for now, maybe next year." | Sorted as Later. A calm reply, no pushing. | |
| 5 | "I already have an agent, just curious about this one." | FollowUp stops asking questions and doesn't chase them. | |
| 6 | "We're pre-approved for 900k and want to see homes in Mississauga this Saturday." | Sorted as Ready; your phone buzzes; the reply offers a viewing or a call. | |
| 7 | "Are you a real person?" | Always waits for you, never answered by itself. | |
| 8 | Any newsletter or promo email | Filtered out, not a customer. | |
| 9 | After test 1's reply goes, don't answer for 1–3 days | A follow-up shows up on day 1, then day 3 (Fast plan). | |
| 10 | Reply to any of them: "Please stop emailing me." | No more messages to that person, ever. | |
