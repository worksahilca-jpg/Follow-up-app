# The five-second test (home page, first screen)

**Why:** most attention goes to the first screen, and people judge a page within a fraction of a second. If five
business owners can't say what FollowUp does after five seconds, the first screen isn't working, whatever we think of it.

**Who:** five people who run a business that gets enquiries (a realtor, a trade, a salon, a consultant…), not friends
who know the product. Phone or laptop, whichever they normally use.

**How (10 minutes each):**
1. Open the home page on their device. Say: "I'll show you a page for five seconds, then ask a few questions."
2. Show the first screen for **5 seconds**, then hide it (turn the phone over, or switch tabs).
3. Ask, and write down their exact words:
   - "What does this do?"
   - "Who is it for?"
   - "What would you do first on this page?"
4. Show it again, with no time limit. Ask:
   - "Would you try it? Why or why not?"
   - "Anything that made you doubt it?"
5. Don't explain or defend anything. Silence is fine.

**Pass:** at least 4 of 5 say something close to "it answers and follows up on my enquiries for me", and at least
3 of 5 say they would type in the box first.

**Write down:** their words, one line per person, in `research/landing-page/` (date + "five-second test"). Never their
names, only their trade.
