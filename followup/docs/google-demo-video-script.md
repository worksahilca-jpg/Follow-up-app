# Google demo video — recording script (2026-10-01)

The video Google's reviewers watch before approving FollowUp's Gmail access. About 3 minutes,
one take, English, narrate out loud (or add captions after). Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**.

**Account:** `sahil@followupbase.io` (a test user in "My First Project", a FollowUp account with
no customers and no Gmail connected yet). One fake enquiry from "Priya" about the 3-bedroom on
Maple Street is already in its inbox.

## Before you press record (not filmed)

1. Use a Chrome window (or Chrome profile) signed in to Google as **sahil@followupbase.io** only.
   Close other tabs. Turn on Do Not Disturb so no notifications pop up.
2. Google account language: **English**.
3. Open three tabs: `followupbase.io`, Gmail (`mail.google.com`), Google Calendar
   (`calendar.google.com`).
4. In the FollowUp tab, if you're signed in as another account, sign out first.
5. **Do not connect Gmail yet.** Connecting is part of the video, because Google wants to see the
   permission screen.

## The recording

| # | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `followupbase.io`. Scroll down slowly to the questions near the bottom, where it says FollowUp reads Gmail. | "This is FollowUp. It watches a business owner's inbox and makes sure no customer is left without a reply." |
| 2 | Click **Sign in** → **Continue with Google** → pick sahil@followupbase.io. | "The owner signs in with Google." |
| 3 | First time only: Settings → **Your business**, change the name to **Maple Realty**, save. (Or do this before recording.) | (nothing) |
| 4 | **Settings → Email → Gmail + Calendar → Connect.** On Google's screen, **stop for 5 seconds** so all permissions can be read. Zoom in (Ctrl/Cmd +) if the text is small. | "FollowUp asks for four things: to read Gmail, to send email, to add calendar events, and to see the email address. Reading finds customer enquiries. Sending replies from the owner's own address. Calendar adds booked calls." |
| 5 | Click **Continue / Allow**. Back in FollowUp, wait until Priya's enquiry appears in **Today** (can take 1–2 minutes; keep recording, or pause and resume). | "FollowUp read the inbox and found a new customer enquiry." |
| 6 | Open Priya's card. Show the drafted reply. Click **Send**. | "It wrote a reply. Nothing goes out until the owner approves. I'll send it." |
| 7 | Switch to the **Gmail** tab → **Sent**. Open the reply. | "Here's that reply in Gmail's Sent folder, sent from the owner's own address." |
| 8 | Back in FollowUp, on Priya's page click **Copy booking link**. Paste it in a new tab, pick any time slot, and book. | "Customers can book a call. Here I'm booking as the customer." |
| 9 | Switch to the **Google Calendar** tab, go to that day, show the event **"Call with Priya"**. | "FollowUp put the call straight onto the owner's Google Calendar." |
| 10 | Back in FollowUp: **Settings → Email → Gmail + Calendar → Disconnect**. | "The owner can disconnect at any time, and can delete all their data in Settings." |

## If something goes wrong

- Priya doesn't show up after 3 minutes: in Settings → Email, click **Sync now**.
- A mistake mid-video: stop, click **Disconnect**, and start again from step 4. Google shows the
  permission screen again.
- Anything else: stop and send me a screenshot.
