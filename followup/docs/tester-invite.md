# Inviting a new tester (2026-10-05)

**Who to ask first** (the Gmail-first study, 2026-10-04): an owner in Ontario whose customer enquiries arrive
by email: a realtor, a contractor, a small home-services business. Best of all, someone who has said "I
forget to get back to people" out loud.

**What we can honestly promise:** every feature free during the beta; nothing goes out without their OK; they
can delete everything any time (Settings → Your data). **What we don't promise:** a price after the beta, or
results. Don't invent either.

**Before you send:** do the three steps in `tester-onboarding-checklist.md` (admin list, Google test users,
Meta tester if they use Instagram). Then send the welcome text at the bottom of that file.

---

## 1. Short version (text, WhatsApp, Instagram DM)

> Hey [name], quick one. I'm building a small tool called FollowUp for people who get customer enquiries by
> email and don't always get back fast. It reads your Gmail, picks out the real customers, writes the reply
> in your own words, and reminds people who went quiet. Nothing sends without your OK.
>
> I'm looking for a few people in Ontario to try it free for two weeks and tell me honestly what's annoying.
> Would you be up for it? Takes about 5 minutes to set up.

## 2. Email version

**Subject:** Can I borrow 5 minutes and your honest opinion?

> Hi [name],
>
> I'm building FollowUp, a small tool for business owners who get customer enquiries by email and don't
> always have time to answer them, or to chase the ones who go quiet.
>

> - It reads your Gmail and picks out the real customers from everything else.
> - It writes a reply in your own words, and you press Send. Nothing goes out without your OK.
> - If a customer goes quiet, it reminds them on day 3, 7, 14 and 30, and stops the moment they answer.
>
> I'm looking for a few owners in Ontario to use it free for two weeks and tell me what's confusing or
> annoying. Setup takes about 5 minutes: sign in with Google and connect your Gmail. You can delete
> everything with one button whenever you like.
>
> If you're up for it, reply with the Google email you'd use and I'll send you the link.
>
> Thanks,
> Sahil

## 3. If they ask "is my email safe?"

> Fair question. FollowUp only reads your inbox to find customer enquiries and write replies for you. To write
> a reply, the customer's message goes to the AI service FollowUp uses (OpenAI), which isn't allowed to train
> on it. Your emails are never used to train AI, never sold, and no reply goes out without your OK. Google will
> show a warning that the app is "unverified" while we're in beta; that's normal for a new app and goes away
> once Google finishes its review. You can disconnect Gmail or delete everything from Settings at any time.

## 4. Two days in (a short nudge if they haven't opened it)

> Hey [name], did FollowUp find anyone waiting in your inbox? If it's quiet, there's a "send yourself a
> practice email" button on the first screen so you can see what it does. Any confusion at all, tell me. That's
> the most useful thing for me right now.

---

**Why it's worded this way:** plain words, no "AI" pitch (`CLAUDE.md`: AI is a capability, never a personality);
the one-sentence promise from the Gmail study; trust before features (brand principle 1); a small, clear ask
(Fogg: make the action easy) and an honest "free for two weeks", not a fake deadline.
