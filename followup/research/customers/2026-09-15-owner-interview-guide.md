# Owner interview guide — validating before building

**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Status:** Guide only. No interviews have been conducted yet — this is a script + tracking
setup for the founder/team to run, not a research finding. Nothing in this file is real
interview data; treat any names/numbers below as placeholders for the template.
**Why this exists:** the pasted external strategy brief (2026-09-15) flagged that every
research pass on file so far (`research/customers/`, `research/market/`) is desk research —
WebSearch snippets and code audits, never a real conversation with a business owner. That's
a genuine gap regardless of what the rest of the brief argued. Target: 10-20 conversations,
per the brief's own suggested range.

## Who to talk to

Pull from FollowUp's actual ICP, not a fresh list — spread across:
- A realtor or small brokerage (existing landing-page persona)
- A freelance consultant (existing landing-page persona)
- A home-services contractor — plumber, HVAC, electrician (flagged in
  `2026-09-05-icp-pain-and-trust-objections.md` as the strongest quantified pain signal
  found, not yet a landing-page persona)
- A med spa / salon / small law-firm intake, if reachable — second cluster from the same file

Aim for at least 3-4 people per bucket so one loud outlier doesn't skew the read.

## Questions

Keep it conversational — this is a script to keep on track, not a form to read verbatim.

**Warm-up / current state**
1. Walk me through what happens right now when a new lead comes in — every channel it
   might arrive on (phone, text, email, DM, walk-in, web form).
2. Who actually answers it, and how fast, on a normal day vs. a busy day?
3. What happens to a lead you don't get back to same-day? Does anyone follow up later, or
   does it just... sit?

**Pain, in their own words**
4. Tell me about a specific lead you know you lost because of follow-up, not because they
   weren't interested. What happened?
5. What's the actual cost of that to you — a number, if you have one (deal size, rough
   monthly leads lost)?
6. Is there a channel you're worse at responding to than others? Which, and why?

**Trust objections — the load-bearing question for this product**
7. If software could reply to a lead on your behalf, automatically, what would make you
   comfortable letting it send without you checking first? What would make you never trust
   it?
8. Have you tried anything like this before (a CRM's auto-responder, an AI tool)? What
   happened — did you keep using it?

**Language / multilingual, if relevant to their business**
9. Do you get leads in a language you don't reply in easily? How do you handle those today?

**Pricing sanity check**
10. What do you currently pay for anything related to this (CRM, answering service, VA)?
    What would "worth it" look like as a monthly price?

**Close**
11. If this worked exactly as described, what would change for you day to day?

## What to do with the answers

Log each conversation in the tracking sheet (see below) right after the call, while it's
fresh — don't batch write-ups. Flag anything that contradicts an existing decision in
`PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md` (e.g., if several home-services owners independently ask for
vertical-specific features) rather than smoothing it over — that's exactly the kind of
signal that would justify revisiting Rule 1, per its own "if new evidence changes the
calculus, that's a fresh CEO decision" clause.

After 10-20 are done, look for: repeated pain phrasing across people (candidate testimonial
language), consistent objections to autonomy (should shape default risk-gate copy), and
whether a single vertical's pain is meaningfully sharper than the others (Rule 1 evidence,
not a foregone conclusion either way).
