# Broader competitive landscape: outside real estate and home services

**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Scope:** FollowUp's competitive set beyond the two verticals already researched
(`2026-09-06-realtor-tool-landscape.md`, `2026-09-08-realtor-competitor-refresh.md`,
`2026-09-08-pricing-validation-home-services-icp.md`). This pass covers (1) general,
horizontal "AI missed-lead / speed-to-lead" tools, (2) the AI SDR / AI sales-agent
category as a possible adjacent threat, and (3) funding/news from the last 6-12
months not already on file. Nothing below re-derives Structurely, Ylopo, Follow Up
Boss, kvCORE/BoldTrail, LionDesk, Wise Agent, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Podium,
Birdeye, CallRail, or Smith.ai — see the files above for those.

## Sourcing caveat

WebFetch is blocked in this sandbox (confirmed again this session). Every finding

## 1. General "AI missed-lead / speed-to-lead" tools (horizontal, not real-estate- or
home-services-locked)

This is the least-researched part of the corpus and the most conceptually adjacent
to FollowUp — several of these companies pitch almost the exact sentence in
`PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md`'s mission statement.

### Beside — the single most important new finding in this pass

Beside is a horizontal AI-receptionist startup for small businesses: answers missed
calls, two-way texts callers who don't get through, books appointments against a
calendar, transfers calls by rule, flags spam, and gives call summaries. It closed a
**$20M Series A led by EQT Ventures (with Index Ventures participating) in November
2025**, on top of a **$10.5M seed led by Index Ventures** — **$32M total** — and
reports **$4M ARR with 20,000+ paying customers** after 18 months in stealth.
Notable angel backer: Stewart Butterfield (Slack founder). *Graded B — funding
figures and investor names corroborated across Fortune, Yahoo Finance/AOL syndication
of the same story, and Dealroom-style aggregators; ARR/customer-count are Beside's
own claim, graded C.*
Sources: [Fortune, 2025-11-11](https://fortune.com/2025/11/11/beside-ai-voice-startup-raises-32-million-ai-receptionist-for-small-business/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/exclusive-beside-ai-voice-startup-130000825.html), [Beside Help Center](https://help.beside.com/en/articles/12784949-getting-started-with-your-ai-receptionist).

**Read:** Beside is real, well-capitalized, horizontal, and squarely aimed at the
same "small business that can't afford a human to do this" buyer FollowUp targets —
not real-estate-locked like Structurely/Ylopo, and not a slow incumbent like
Salesforce. But everything found describes it as **reactive and in-the-moment**:
answer the call now, text back now, book the appointment now. No feature found
(across its own help center, review sites, or press coverage) describing proactive
re-engagement of a lead that went quiet *days* after first contact, or a
cross-channel (email + social + SMS) neglect score. That gap — the "lead already
answered once, then died in silence" problem — is exactly FollowUp's Point 2
(rescue) and is not evidenced anywhere in Beside's public feature set. This is the
best-funded genuine threat found in this pass; see Part 2 for what it changes.

### Bravi — YC-backed, home-services-adjacent, explicitly "never miss a lead"

Bravi (YC F25, founded 2025 by Anas Bouassami and Pierre-Habté Nouvellon) markets
itself as "the AI operating system for home-services businesses," starting in
fenestration (shutters, windows, garage doors) in Europe and the US before expanding
to adjacent trades. Its own positioning: installers and manufacturers "lose 30–40% of
potential revenue because they miss calls, don't answer emails, and fail to respond
to chats in time," and Bravi "replaces the front office... with AI agents that never
miss a lead." *Graded C — YC's own launch page and the company's site; no independent
funding amount found beyond YC's standard-deal implication.*
Sources: [YC launch page](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Obq-bravi-ai-voice-and-chat-assistants-for-home-service-companies), [bravi.app](https://www.bravi.app/).

**Read:** this is the closest single sentence-level match to FollowUp's own mission
statement found in this research pass — but it is European-founded, home-services-
vertical-locked (not horizontal like FollowUp), and still small (YC F25, no Series A
found). It reinforces rather than surprises: it's more evidence, alongside
Structurely/Ylopo, that "AI that never misses a lead" is an active, fundable pitch
multiple teams are independently building toward — just always inside one vertical,
never horizontal. Nobody found in this pass, including Bravi, is horizontal.

### Leaping AI — YC-backed voice-AI, enterprise/BPO-leaning, not small-business self-serve

Leaping AI (Germany-founded 2023, relocated to Silicon Valley) closed a **$4.7M seed**
led by Nexus Venture Partners with Y Combinator and Paul Graham participating. Its
voice agents handle scheduling, billing, and lead qualification/re-engagement at
volume (10,000+ calls/day claimed) for retail, telecom, BPO, and travel clients —
i.e., mid-market/enterprise call-center replacement, not a $29-99/mo small-business
self-serve product. *Graded C, vendor and press-release-adjacent sources.*
Sources: [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/feed/leaping-ai-raises-4-7m-faces-challenges), [Menlo Times](https://www.menlotimes.com/post/leaping-ai-raised-4-7-million-to-bring-ai-voice-agents-to-companies-and-call-centers).

**Read:** conceptually similar (voice AI that qualifies and follows up on leads) but
a different buyer and price tier — enterprise call-center replacement, not a solo
plumber or realtor signing up with a credit card. Low direct-competition risk to
FollowUp's actual ICP.

### Verse.ai — the closest horizontal analog that already exists, and has for years

Verse.ai (founded 2013, San Diego; total raised **$18.85M**, last round ~6 years ago,
Series A backer Silicon Valley Bank) runs conversational AI agents across SMS, email,
voicemail, webchat, and voice, responding in under 15 seconds and **following up for
up to six months** — a near-exact match to FollowUp's "rescue leads that went cold"
framing, and multi-channel rather than single-channel. It is most used by real
estate/mortgage teams but markets itself as usable by "business owners" generally.
Pricing requires a sales call, an annual contract (+5% for quarterly), and a setup
fee — not a flat self-serve price. *Graded C, aggregator/review-site sourced.*
Sources: [Verse.ai](https://verse.ai/), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/178756/Verse/), [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/agentology).

**Read:** Verse.ai is the strongest evidence in this whole pass that "multi-channel
AI that follows up on a lead for months until it converts or dies" is not a novel
idea — it has existed, funded, for over a decade, just under-the-radar (no major
raise since ~2019) and sales-gated rather than self-serve. It has not displaced the
generalist CRMs or become a household name in 12+ years of operating, which is a
data point for "this space is real but hard to break out in," not "this space is
already won."

### Numa and Goodcall — narrower, vertical or feature-limited

**Numa** is SMS-first missed-call follow-up, but built specifically for automotive
dealerships, not general small business. **Goodcall** is a general small-business AI
phone agent (FAQs, appointment booking, basic lead capture via CRM integrations) —
closer to Beside in shape but smaller and less funded in everything found. Neither
shows evidence of cross-channel dormant-lead revival or a neglect score.
Sources: [Numa](https://www.numa.com/), [Goodcall](https://www.goodcall.com/), [PYREXA Numa alternatives](https://pyrexa.ai/numa-alternatives).

### The "Lead Rescue" name itself is already generic and commoditized

Searching the literal phrase "lead rescue" surfaces **at least seven unrelated small
sites** using it as a product or campaign name — leadrescue.co ("Sales Powered AI"),
leadrescue.app (an AI-search-visibility tool, unrelated to missed leads),
leadrescueai1.com, leadrescue.net, myleadrescue.com (by "AiQuote"), lead-rescue.com,
and mergerank.ai's "AI Lead Rescue for Home Services" page — none of which appear to
be venture-funded or have any scale evidence; most read as local-SEO-agency or
marketing-consultant branded offerings bundling a missed-call-text-back feature.
*Graded C, WebSearch result-listing only — not independently verified for any of
these beyond the search-result snippet.*
Sources: search results for `leadrescue.co OR leadrescue.app OR myleadrescue.com`, accessed via WebSearch 2026-09-08.

**Read:** this is worth flagging for the CEO/growth-agent specifically — "lead
rescue" as a marketing phrase is already crowded and somewhat devalued by a swarm of
small, low-quality, look-alike offerings. FollowUp's actual product is well ahead of
what any of these appear to be, but the phrase itself won't read as distinctive in
paid search or SEO; the product's mechanism (the score, the audit trail, the report)
needs to carry the differentiation, not the phrase "rescue."

## 2. AI SDR / AI sales-agent category — 11x, Artisan, Regie.ai, AiSDR

**Verdict: not a credible threat to FollowUp's niche today — different market,
different buyer, different motion.**

- **Pricing tier:** a realistic year-one deployment of 11x or Artisan runs
  **$60,000-$100,000**, once platform license, supporting tooling, and human
  oversight time are included — two to three orders of magnitude above FollowUp's
  $29/mo. These are enterprise/mid-market sales-org purchases with an annual
  contract, not small-business self-serve.
- **Motion:** the category's core job is **outbound prospecting** — researching
  target accounts, finding contacts, writing personalized cold outreach to people
  who have never engaged — the opposite motion from FollowUp's job (an *inbound*
  lead already contacted the business; the problem is nobody followed up). Some
  platforms (11x, Artisan) also handle inbound qualification as a secondary feature,
  but it's not the product's center of gravity or its buyer's reason for purchase.
- **Reality check found in the research itself:** even inside this well-funded
  category, "the 'autonomous AI SDR' that does outbound while you sleep has largely
  failed in practice; the 'AI-assisted human SDR' that ships 3x the work... is what
  actually moves pipeline" — i.e., the category's own commentary is skeptical of the
  full-autonomy claim FollowUp is explicitly building toward for its own (much
  narrower, inbound-only) job.
- **Watch item, not an action item:** AiSDR is described as "the most SMB-friendly on
  price and contract flexibility" of the group — the one name in this category
  showing any downmarket drift. Nothing found suggests it or any peer is targeting a
  $29-99/mo self-serve small-business buyer today, but it's the one to re-check in a
  future pass if the category's pricing continues to compress.

Sources: [11x guide: Artisan vs Regie.ai vs 11x](https://www.11x.ai/guides/artisan-vs-regie-ai-vs-11x), [Layer3Labs AI SDR for small business](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/ai-sdr-for-small-business), [Qualified: inbound vs outbound AI SDRs](https://www.qualified.com/plus/articles/understanding-the-difference-between-inbound-and-outbound-ai-sdrs), [Fundraise Insider AI SDR tools 2026](https://fundraiseinsider.com/blog/ai-sdr-tools/).

## 4. What this changes, in one paragraph

The realtor and home-services passes already on file found FollowUp's pitch
crowded *inside those two verticals specifically*. This pass finds the same pitch —
"AI that never misses or lets a lead go cold" — is **also being built horizontally**,
independently, by multiple funded teams (most seriously Beside, at $32M and 20K+
customers) who are not vertical-locked and are targeting the same small-business
buyer FollowUp is. None of them, as far as this pass could verify, do the specific
thing FollowUp's own gap analysis (`2026-09-07-lead-rescue-gap-and-strategy.md`)
identified as the real moat — proactive, cross-channel detection that a lead who
*was* answered has since gone quiet, expressed as a score, with a recovered-revenue
report behind it. That gap appears to still be open. But "still open" is now a
claim with a clock on it, not an indefinite one — see the synthesis doc for what
follows from that. The AI SDR category, by contrast, is confirmed as a non-threat:
different buyer, different price tier, different motion (outbound vs. inbound).
