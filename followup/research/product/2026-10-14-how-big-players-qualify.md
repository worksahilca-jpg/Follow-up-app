# How the big players qualify real-estate leads, and the structure FollowUp takes from them

**Date:** 2026-10-14. **Question (founder):** *"Is this a proven system with the big companies or the big REsimpli
traders? What do they do? I want to know how they qualify the leads, so we're going to make a structure based on
that."* **Outcome:** approved as A-246 (design brain).

Builds on `2026-10-09-lead-qualification-strategy.md` (evidence-quoted checklist, golden set) and
`2026-09-09-followup-cadence-best-practices.md` (follow-up days). Does not repeat them.

## Confidence key

- **[OWN]**: the company's own page or help centre.
- **[THIRD]**: a review, trade press or vendor blog about the company. Treat as secondhand.
- **[ADVICE]**: a trainer's or vendor's advice, not a measured result.

## 1. What each one checks

**Honest headline:** none of them publishes its script or its scoring rules. What they *check* is public, and they
all check the same few things.

### Buyers

| Who | What they check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Zillow Premier Agent | Timeline to buy (0–3 months up to 12+ months) and whether the buyer can talk now; live connections are vetted on timeline. Financing interest is handled separately (pre-approval outreach). A buyer not ready to talk becomes a "nurture" lead. Agents see pre-approval status and time frame on the lead. | [OWN] |
| Ylopo rAIya (AI text/voice) | Timeline, budget, pre-approval (some reviews add motivation). Texts about listings the lead actually searched. Its own FAQ says it does not try to set the appointment; it warms the lead until they're ready. Reviews disagree on whether it books. | [OWN] for the FAQ, [THIRD] for the criteria |
| Structurely (AI ISA "Holmes") | Responds within a minute, answers the lead's questions and asks its own: area, timeline, pre-approved or cash, already working with an agent. Hot / Warm / Cold configurable. Mainly SMS. No public script. | [THIRD] |
| LPMAMA (the checklist trainers teach agents and ISAs) | Location, Price, Motivation, Agent, Mortgage, Appointment. The order is flexible and follows the conversation. Origin not established (not shown to be Keller Williams). | [ADVICE] |
| Redfin | Nothing public on how it screens buyers. Partner agents pay a referral fee on close. | none usable |

### Sellers

| Who | What they check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| REsimpli (investor CRM) | Four things: motivation (a life event creating urgency), timeline (30 days is a different lead from "sometime this year"), condition, price expectation. Strong on all four = hot. Motivation and timeline but an unrealistic price = nurture until the expectation shifts. No urgency and no flexibility = long nurture or dead. | [OWN] |
| Opendoor (online seller flow) | Address, home details, condition room by room, timeline (ASAP, 2–4 weeks, 4–6 weeks, 6+ weeks, just browsing). | [OWN] |

### How agents sort leads by time

90 days = high motivation, 3–6 months = medium, 6+ months = low (HousingWire). Pre-approval is the filter trainers
name most (FullEnrich, Thoughtly). [THIRD]

### How many questions a message

- Email: 1–3 questions get about 50% more replies than none; 3 do better than 8+ (Boomerang, 2016, its own data).
- Text: texting guides advise one ask per text and warn that one text asking area, budget, timing and financing gets
  ignored (GoCRM, Textus). [ADVICE]
- Nothing found from the big companies on questions per message. "Two a message, joined into one sentence" is
  FollowUp's choice between the email data and the texting advice.

## 2. The pattern they share

1. **When comes first.** Zillow sorts on it; everyone asks it.
2. **Money only for people moving soon** (Zillow handles financing separately; trainers make pre-approval the filter).
3. **Already with an agent?** (Structurely, LPMAMA's A.)
4. **Sellers: why, condition, price in mind** (REsimpli, Opendoor).
5. **Don't push a meeting on someone not ready; keep following up** (Ylopo, Zillow's nurture).

## 3. The structure FollowUp takes (A-246)

- Buyer or seller first (from the first message, or ask).
- **Buyer:** area + when → (moving within 6 months) pre-approved or cash + price range, otherwise kind of home → with
  an agent yet + see the home.
- **Seller:** address + when → why + condition → price in mind + a visit to give a real number.
- **Ready** (≤3 months + money sorted; seller ≤3 months with a reason) buzzes the owner. **Warm** (3–6 months or
  money not sorted) → Fast plan. **Later** (6+ months / just looking) → Long plan. **Has an agent** → stop and tell
  the owner. **No** → stop.

What is proven is what they check and the order. The exact words and the two-question rule are ours, and the success
figures these companies publish (Ylopo's 48% text response, Structurely's qualification rates) are their own claims,
not verified.
